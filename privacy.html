Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Post Manager (“we,” “our,” or “the Service”) is intended to handle information when you use the application and this website. The actual data handling must be reviewed against the version of the application you use.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and authorization data: basic account identifiers and authorization tokens made available through the official sign-in and API flows of connected platforms.
  • Content and publishing data: video files you select, captions, hashtags, scheduling choices, and publishing status needed to provide the requested workflow.
  • Technical information: diagnostic logs and basic device or application information needed to troubleshoot errors and maintain security.
  • Support information: information you choose to include when you contact support.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, provide requested content-management and publishing features, display account or publishing status, troubleshoot problems, protect the Service, and respond to support requests.

3. Platform data

When you connect TikTok, Facebook, or another platform, that platform may provide information according to the permissions you approve. Your use of those platforms remains subject to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not use platform data for purposes unrelated to providing or maintaining the features you request.

4. Storage and retention

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. The exact storage location and retention period depend on the application version and its configuration. Before using the Service with real accounts, review the app's current settings and documentation to understand where local content, logs, and authorization data are stored.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a connected platform when you explicitly request an account, upload, or publishing action. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the security and rights of users or the Service.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Never share your passwords, access tokens, or verification codes with support or other people.

7. Your choices

You can stop using the Service, revoke its access through the relevant platform's account settings, and remove application data using the controls provided by your device and the app. Revoking access may prevent connected features from working.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please visit our Support page.
